Drop your images in this folder (jpg / png / jpeg / webp). Anything missing falls back to the built-in abstract art.

  cover.jpg          front cover (outside)             ratio 1.37 : 1.9   e.g. 1370x1900
  cover-inside.jpg   inside of the front cover
  page-01.jpg ... page-20.jpg   the paper pages, in reading order   ratio 13 : 18   e.g. 1300x1800
  back-inside.jpg    inside of the back cover
  back.jpg           back cover (outside)
  spine.jpg          spine (optional)

Page 1 is the right-hand page you see when the cover opens; pages 2-3 appear after the first turn, and so on.
Higher resolution = sharper close-ups (2600x3600 pages are fine).
